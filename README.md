# ClipBridge

Copy on your Mac or PC, paste on your Android phone or tablet — a shared clipboard for your own
devices, end-to-end encrypted.

ClipBridge links the devices you already use into one clipboard: copy text on the Mac and it is
already on your Android device's clipboard; send text back with one tap. Everything is encrypted
end to end (AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from a passphrase the app generates for you) — the relay
only forwards ciphertext and deletes each copy as soon as your devices have it. No history, nothing
to mine.

An account is optional. Sign in with Google and a new device joins your group as soon as you confirm
the same 4-digit code on one you already have; see and remove your devices at
[keepware.dev/account](https://www.keepware.dev/account). The account never sees what you copy.

## Downloads

Get the latest release from the website (downloads are counted there):

**https://www.keepware.dev/clipbridge**

- macOS 13+ — one universal app (Apple silicon & Intel)
- Windows 10/11 — on the Microsoft Store (x64 and ARM64), or a standalone .exe
- Linux — Ubuntu 22.04 and later (Wayland or Xorg), a .deb for x64 and ARM64
- Android 8.0+ — on Google Play, one app for phones and tablets

Release notes live under [Releases](https://github.com/keepwarehq/clipbridge/releases).
