# ClipBridge

Copy on your Mac, paste on your Android tablet or phone — a shared clipboard for your own devices,
end-to-end encrypted.

ClipBridge links the devices you already use into one clipboard: copy text on the Mac and it is
already on your Android device's clipboard; send text back with one tap. Everything is encrypted
end to end (AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2 key from your passphrase) — the relay only forwards ciphertext
and forgets each copy as soon as your devices have it. No account, no history, nothing to mine.
